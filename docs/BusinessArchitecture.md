# Business Architecture

## Business Objectives
- Digitize agricultural insurance operations for national scale
- Improve underwriting accuracy and claims turnaround time
- Increase transparency for government and partner institutions
- Provide auditable, secure, and GIS-aware decision support

## Core Business Capabilities
1. Farmer and farm onboarding
2. Crop and season management
3. Policy issuance and premium collection
4. Risk assessment and intelligence
5. Claims intake and payout management
6. Reporting, audit, and administration

## Key Actors
- Farmers
- Field officers
- Insurance administrators
- Finance teams
- Government oversight agencies
- Partner organizations

## Business Processes
- Registration and verification
- Policy underwriting
- Premium collection
- Risk monitoring
- Claim submission and adjudication
- Payout processing
- Audit and compliance review
